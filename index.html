Board Setup & Objective

Damma is played on an 8x8 grid with Player 1 (Gold 🟡) starting on rows 5–7 and Player 2 (Dark 🔴) starting on rows 0–2.

💡 Objective: Capture all opponent pieces or block them so they have no legal moves left!
